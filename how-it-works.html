How It Works

Step 1: Enter Your Measurements

Provide your bust, waist, hips, height, and sleeve length measurements. The more accurate your measurements, the better the fit!

Step 2: Choose Your Garment

Select the type of garment you want to create, such as a dress, skirt, blouse, pants, or jacket. You can also choose your preferred fabric type.

Step 3: Generate Your Pattern

Our AI will generate a custom sewing pattern based on your measurements and garment choice. You'll see a preview of your pattern before you download it.

Step 4: Download and Sew

Download your pattern as a PDF, print it out, and start sewing! Your custom-fit garment is just a few steps away.
